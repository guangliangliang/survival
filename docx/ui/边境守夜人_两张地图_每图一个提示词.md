# 《边境守夜人》地图 AI 图片提示词

## 统一要求

- 文件命名规则：全部使用英文小写字母和下划线，避免中文、空格和特殊符号

- 游戏类型：俯视角生存射击小游戏
- 地图形式：纯平面地图
- 玩家可以在整张地图内自由移动
- 推荐比例：16:9
- 推荐生成尺寸：5120 × 2880
- 推荐最终格式：WebP 或 PNG
- 镜头视角：固定 3/4 斜俯视
- 光照方向：统一从左上方照射
- 地图中不要直接画入岩石、沙袋、围栏、木箱，这些资源后续使用独立透明 PNG 单独摆放
- 不要出现坡道、高台、沟渠、悬崖、楼梯、建筑内部或真实高度差
- 画面需要适合移动端游戏，保证道路、区域和角色走位清晰可读

# 地图一：荒漠边境平原

## 推荐图片文件名

```text
map_frontier_desert_plain.png
```

## 中文说明

这是一张开阔、平坦的荒漠边境地图，适合作为第一关或动物怪物关卡。

地图中央为大面积沙地战斗区域；北部通过轮胎印、脚印、地面压痕和磨损痕迹表现边境活动区域；西侧是一条宽阔土路；东侧是颜色略深的荒地；南部是一条横向边境道路。

所有区域必须自然连通，不能出现死胡同和封闭区域。岩石、沙袋、围栏和木箱不直接画进地图背景。

## AI 图片提示词

```text
Create one large flat top-down 2.5D game map for a mobile survivor shooter, set in an arid frontier wasteland. The entire terrain must be completely flat, fully walkable, and designed for free player movement across the whole map. Use a fixed three-quarter top-down game camera with no strong perspective distortion. Place a broad open sandy combat area in the center. In the northern area, show only subtle ground traces of frontier activity, including tire tracks, footprints, disturbed sand, faded ground marks, and worn patches, but do not include any actual props, structures, or obstacles. Add a wide dusty dirt road along the western side. Make the eastern side a slightly darker barren ground zone with sparse dry vegetation textures and small surface details painted into the ground. Add a flat border road across the southern area. Connect every region with wide open paths and avoid dead ends or enclosed spaces. Use muted sand, brown, dusty gray, and faded frontier colors, with consistent soft light from the upper left, subtle baked ground shading, clear gameplay readability, detailed but uncluttered textures, and a wide 16:9 composition. Do not include standalone rocks, sandbags, fences, wooden crates, barrels, vehicles, buildings, walls, characters, enemies, UI, text, labels, logos, or watermarks.
```

# 地图三：红土边境平原

## 推荐图片文件名

```text
map_frontier_red_earth_plain.png
```

## 中文说明

这是一张红褐色硬土地面的边境平原地图，不属于荒漠，也不是草原，整体风格更加粗犷、硬朗。

地图中央为开阔红土战斗区；北部使用轮胎印、巡逻路线和浅色磨损痕迹表现边境活动；西侧是一条宽阔红土道路；东侧地面更加粗糙，带少量碎石纹理和干草；南部是一条平坦边境道路。

地图主色调为红褐色、砖红色、浅棕色和少量干草黄色，适合强盗或中后期敌人关卡。

```text
Create one large flat top-down 2.5D game map for a mobile survivor shooter, set in a red-earth frontier plain. The entire terrain must be completely flat, fully walkable, and designed for free player movement across the whole map. Use a fixed three-quarter top-down game camera with no strong perspective distortion. Place a broad open combat area in the center, made of compact hard red soil mixed with dusty patches, subtle surface cracks, and occasional short dry grass textures. In the northern area, show subtle traces of a frontier patrol route using light wheel marks, worn red soil, faded route impressions, and compacted ground, but do not include any actual props or obstacles. Add a wide red dirt road along the western side. Make the eastern side a slightly rougher red-earth zone with small stone textures painted into the ground and sparse dry grass patches. Add a flat border road across the southern area. Connect every region with wide open paths and avoid dead ends or enclosed spaces. Use red-brown, brick red, dusty tan, muted brown, and small hints of dry yellow grass, with consistent soft light from the upper left, subtle baked ground shading, strong gameplay readability, detailed but uncluttered terrain textures, and a wide 16:9 composition. ```
```
